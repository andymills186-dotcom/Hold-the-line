HOLD THE LINE

Swarmers flood the canyon road from both ends to overrun the command post. Hold both barricades, earn supply and build your arsenal between waves.

SHOOTING

FIELD MANUAL

  • CONTROLSLeft thumb moves. Drag your right thumb to look
  • FIREAutomatic when the crosshair is on a bug
  • GUNSAll automatic. Tap the ammo panel to swap to your SMG
  • THROWRound button. NEXT switches frag, mine, fire and C4
  • SUPPLYKills earn points. Spend them between waves on guns, gun towers and gear
  • AIRFrom wave 2, kills charge the air support button
  • REVIVEYour four squadmates revive each other. Follow the red marker to help, and stand next to them

On a computer: click to capture the mouse, WASD to move, click to fire, Q to swap to your SMG, G or right-click to throw, T for the next throwable, R to reload, F to mount a tower, V for air support, B for the supply menu, Esc to release.
